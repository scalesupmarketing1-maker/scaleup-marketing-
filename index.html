FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Scale Up Marketing.

Do I need any prior experience to join? +

No prior experience is required at all. Our courses are designed specifically for beginners. We start from absolute zero and take you step-by-step to running a profitable online business. Whether you're a student, housewife, or professional — everyone is welcome.

Are classes available online or only in-person? +

We offer both! You can join our offline physical classes at Al Latif Center, Gulberg III, Lahore, or attend our live online sessions from anywhere in Pakistan or abroad. The curriculum is identical for both formats.

What is the Investor Store Management Program? +

This program is for investors who want to earn from e-commerce without managing it themselves. Our team handles everything — store setup, product research, ads, fulfilment, and scaling. You invest, we operate, and both parties share the profits. Book a free consultation to learn more.

Which countries does your dropshipping program cover? +

Our dropshipping program covers UAE, Saudi Arabia (KSA), Pakistan, United Kingdom, and the United States. We help with international product research, supplier and fulfilment setup, and running Meta and TikTok ad campaigns in each market.

Do you offer a free consultation before enrolling? +

Yes! We offer a completely free consultation to help you figure out which program or service is the right fit for your goals and budget. You can call us at 0328-0814301, email us, or visit our office in Gulberg III, Lahore.

What is the paid internship program? +

Our paid internship is a hands-on practical training program where you work on real projects and real clients under the supervision of our expert team. Interns get stipends and build a strong portfolio. It's ideal for fresh graduates or students wanting real-world e-commerce experience.
